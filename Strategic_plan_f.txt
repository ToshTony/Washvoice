Strategic Plan Executive Summary: WaSHVoice Strategic Plan (2025–2027)Since its inception in 2018, WaSHVoice has evolved from a burgeoning social enterprise into a pivotal force within Kenya’s Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WaSH) landscape. Headquartered in Nairobi, the organization operates at the intersection of advocacy, sector coordination, and climate action, purposefully designed to dismantle the silos that traditionally hinder service delivery. This Strategic Plan (2025–2027) serves as a bold three-year roadmap aimed at bridging the gap between high-level policy and community-level impact. By leveraging pooled, blended capital and data-driven insights, WaSHVoice is committed to securing the sanitation value chain and ensuring every Kenyan has the agency to influence the services essential to their health and dignity.Our strategic framework is built upon four non-negotiable pillars: Advocacy & Justice, Capacity Building, Research & Evidence, and Climate Resilience. These pillars address a critical economic imperative, as sanitation inaction currently drains the Kenyan economy of KES 27 Billion annually. To counter this, WaSHVoice will position itself as a central "knowledge hub," utilizing our niche expertise in inclusion to unify fragmented sector actors. Through evidence-based justice and ecological stewardship, we aim to transform sanitation from a neglected service into a governed, resilient ecosystem that can withstand the increasing volatility of climate change.The roadmap to 2027 is structured in three progressive phases: Foundational Mapping, Capacity Scaling, and Investment Attraction. In 2025, we will launch the National Sanitation Directory and secure catalytic "Sanifund" investments to anchor the sector. This will be followed by the 2026 establishment of Professional Standards Certifications and a Sani-Entrepreneurship Hub to professionalize the workforce. By 2027, the focus shifts to systemic expansion, aiming to unlock 10x matching debt through blended finance models and real-time monitoring via the National Sanitation Database 2.0. Ultimately, we hold ourselves accountable to a rigorous ROI target, seeking to generate $5.50 of economic value for every $1 invested, ensuring that every intervention translates into tangible national progress.Introduction/Background1.00: Strategic Framework: Advocacy, Equity, and ResilienceIn the six years since its founding in 2018, WaSHVoice has moved beyond the "nascent" stage to become a key influencer in Kenya’s WaSH landscape.
