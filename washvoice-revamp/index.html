  • Strategic Plan Executive Summary: WaSHVoice Strategic Plan (2025–2027)Since its inception in 2018, WaSHVoice has evolved from a burgeoning social enterprise into a pivotal force within Kenya’s Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WaSH) landscape.
  • This Strategic Plan (2025–2027) serves as a bold three-year roadmap aimed at bridging the gap between high-level policy and community-level impact.
  • By leveraging pooled, blended capital and data-driven insights, WaSHVoice is committed to securing the sanitation value chain and ensuring every Kenyan has the agency to influence the services essential to their health and dignity.Our strategic framework is built upon four non-negotiable pillars: Advocacy & Justice, Capacity Building, Research & Evidence, and Climate Resilience.
  • These pillars address a critical economic imperative, as sanitation inaction currently drains the Kenyan economy of KES 27 Billion annually.
  • To counter this, WaSHVoice will position itself as a central "knowledge hub," utilizing our niche expertise in inclusion to unify fragmented sector actors.
  • Through evidence-based justice and ecological stewardship, we aim to transform sanitation from a neglected service into a governed, resilient ecosystem that can withstand the increasing volatility of climate change.The roadmap to 2027 is structured in three progressive phases: Foundational Mapping, Capacity Scaling, and Investment Attraction.

Section 2

Water Sanitation and Hygiene Voice (WaSHVoice)

(WaSHVoice) is committed to bringing social change through training, improving facilities according to community needs, and protecting the environment for future generations.

Founded in 2018, WaSHVoice is a social enterprise that strengthens the voice of the vulnerable in water and sanitation to promote inclusivity and justice in service delivery. It connects diverse knowledge in strategic management and accountability in WaSH for human health, dignity and ecological footprint.

Our inspiration is to build an effective Water, Sanitation and Hygiene relationship between service providers, users and other stakeholders by providing expertise and interventions to improve health and access to water, sanitation and hygiene services
