Strategic Interventions

Transforming WaSH Through
Targeted Action.

We design, coordinate, and execute specialized services across four non-negotiable pillars to secure Kenya's sanitation value chain and foster resilient communities.

Pillar 01

Advocacy, Justice & Communication

We drive accountability in the WaSH sector to transform basic access into a just, inclusive, and rights-based service delivery model.

  • Social Change Communication: Strengthening the voice of the vulnerable to promote inclusivity and justice in service delivery.
  • Policy Alignment: Influencing new Sanitation Guidelines and presenting empirical evidence for rights-based service delivery at national summits.
  • Empowering Marginalized Groups: Supporting community-led WaSH service definition so locals are co-creators, not just recipients.
Pillar 02

Capacity Building & Training

We cultivate expert leadership and technical proficiency to design and manage resilient systems, ensuring the long-term stewardship of high-impact WaSH infrastructure.

  • Professional Standards Certification: Establishing industry-led voluntary standards for self-regulation and excellence in the sanitation workforce.
  • Sani-Entrepreneurship Hub: Training SMEs and micro-entrepreneurs on financial literacy, technical maintenance, and record-keeping.
  • Institutional Strengthening: Providing expertise and interventions to improve health and access for Water Service Providers (WSPs).
Pillar 03

Research, Monitoring & Evaluation

We leverage data-driven insights to challenge legacy systems. By synthesizing human narratives with rigorous quantitative data, we transform sanitation into a governed ecosystem.

  • National Sanitation Directory (NSD): Aggregating empirical data on providers, skills, and capacity to act as a definitive sector resource.
  • Real-time M&E: Deploying NSD 2.0 for real-time monitoring and benchmarking of service delivery across counties.
  • Sectoral Empirical Analysis: Providing investors and donors with high ROI tracking ($5.50 value per $1 invested) through clear metrics.
Pillar 04

Ecological Stewardship & Climate Resilience

We operationalize ecological stewardship by transitioning from static infrastructure to dynamic, circular sanitation systems capable of withstanding escalating climate volatility.

  • Climate-Smart Infrastructure: Improving facilities according to community needs while actively protecting the environment.
  • Circular Sanitation Systems: Implementing waste-to-energy and composting solutions to build a resilient ecological footprint.
  • Technology Anthology: Publishing guides on resilient technologies to help institutions navigate climate adaptation funding.

Our Methodology

How We Engage.

Our approach is deeply contextual. We tailor our engagement strategies to ensure synergy across the entire WaSH ecosystem.

Local Communities

We utilize Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) methods. We ensure communities are not just recipients, but co-creators of the solutions, mapping infrastructure to fit cultural and physical needs.

Donors & Investors

We focus on Data Visualization and transparency. Investors see the tangible voice of the organization through clear metrics, KPI tracking, and human-centric storytelling to de-risk investments.

Government & Institutions

We prioritize Policy Alignment. We ensure that all WaSHVoice activities are seamlessly integrated into existing district and national water master plans to meet SDG 6 development goals.
